## Shared contract boundary

- L: session cookie and CSRF on mutations (`server.py:1740-1754`); SQLite `storyboard.db` under `STORYBOARD_DATA_ROOT`, with command modules and project bundles. Anonymous health and share routes are exceptions. L remains the configured runtime.
- V: bearer JWT on business routes via `apps/api/app/api/v1/deps.py`; `AsyncSession` and SQLAlchemy models use `DATABASE_URL` (`apps/api/app/core/database.py`). Anonymous health and share GET are exceptions. V routes exist but are **IMPLEMENTED_NOT_INTEGRATED** until actual clients and deployment switch.
- F: Legacy session cookie or bearer token via `fastapi_app/dependencies.py`, direct SQLite connection and `SQLiteUnitOfWork`; presence routes do not depend on that authentication. `fastapi_app/main.py:57-62` mounts F, but the inspected startup paths do not start it; `storyboard-system/tests/test_fastapi_app.py` directly constructs its app. Classify F as **test-only parallel implementation**, not the running owner.

Every V path in the table is the **CANONICAL target**, not a cut-over runtime route. `DUP` = parallel route; `DIFF` = similar purpose, different method, shape, auth, mutation, or storage; `MISS` = no V route in `apps/api/app/api/v1`; `V-ONLY` = V route without a matching L route. ## Prioritized next gates

1. **Read route before write route:** V-Web 已真实使用 `/api/v1/productions/{id}/export/{edl|otio|srt|vtt|csv}`；SRT/VTT 有字节级 Legacy 时间码合同，浏览器门禁会验证 VTT 的真实下载入口；状态仍仅 `INTEGRATED_NOT_CUT_OVER`。`tests/backend/test_exports_srt.py` 仍只覆盖 V 路由，下一步需在隔离环境比较 L/V 完整输出并核对真实部署入口。
2. **Shot command parity:** compare L single/bulk commands with V patch/bulk on 409, no-op, audit, panels and deletes; do not switch writers until one transaction owner exists. `tests/backend/test_productions_shots.py` is a basic V pipeline, not L parity.
3. **Missing persistence:** inventory project bundle/fields/media/import consumers, then add one bounded V route family at a time. Rehearse SQLite→PostgreSQL on a copy with row, reference, media and rollback checks before switching storage.
4. **Retire parallel tree after evidence:** F is currently test-only and its export routes are broken; remove only after confirming no deployment/client import and transferring any unique behavior. `storyboard-system/tests/test_fastapi_app.py` tests health/OpenAPI/presence/auth protection, not route parity.

No route in this matrix is claimed `CUT_OVER` or `LEGACY_RETIRED`.
